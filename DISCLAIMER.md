## Intended use

DiscMount is a media player and file browser for optical drives. It is meant for playing and reading **discs you legally own**, for
personal use, on your own device.

## Copy-protection circumvention

Playing commercial DVD-Video and Blu-ray discs requires handling copy-protection systems (CSS and AACS). In some countries, circumventing
technical protection measures is restricted or illegal even for personal use or when you own the disc (for example, section 1201 of the US DMCA, and
the national laws implementing Article 6 of EU Directive 2001/29/EC). Others allow it for private use or interoperability.

* **You are solely responsible** for checking and complying with the laws that apply where you live and use the software.
* DiscMount **does not include, ship, download or link to any decryption keys, device keys, host certificates or `KEYDB.cfg` files.**
  Blu-ray (AACS) decryption works only if **you** supply a key file for discs you own.
* DiscMount is not designed or intended to copy, rip, record or redistribute protected content, and the authors do not support that.
* If you cannot lawfully use these features where you live, do not build or use the CSS/AACS parts of the software.
